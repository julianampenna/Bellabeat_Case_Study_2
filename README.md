# Bellabeat Case Study — Fitbit Activity Analysis

### Overview
This ongoing project is part of the Google Data Analytics Certificate. It explores Fitbit users' activity patterns to identify potential marketing opportunities for Bellabeat's Leaf wellness tracker.

### Data and Tools
Two independent Fitbit datasets were analyzed using **Python** for data cleaning and **Google BigQuery (SQL)** for data validation and analysis.

After excluding zero-step records, unusually high step counts, duplicates, and participants with fewer than seven valid recorded days, the analysis included **69 participants** across both datasets.

### Key Findings
- Fitbit users exhibited considerable variation in average daily activity levels.
- In the original sample, **30%** averaged fewer than 5,000 steps daily, compared with **2.6%** in the Norwegian sample.
- **26.7%** of original participants and **46.2%** of Norwegian participants averaged at least 10,000 steps daily.
- Differences in sample composition and recording periods limit direct comparisons.

### Marketing Recommendation
Bellabeat could test **personalized activity goals and progressive wellness milestones** for Leaf, emphasizing individual progress rather than a universal 10,000-step target.

A proposed *Your Wellness Journey* campaign could incorporate achievement-based messaging inspired by gamification. Its effectiveness would require customer research and A/B testing.

### Next Steps
Follow-up would include: sleep analysis, sensitivity testing, and additional marketing recommendations.
